Build and defend your base to survive 50 waves. Use the power of the spectrum to empower your defences.

Buildings:
- Solar plant: make energy by day, less effective at night
- Energy Link: routes energy throughout your base
- Miner: uses energy to mine rocks for resources
- Tower: uses energy to attack enemies

Defence towers start quite weak. Feed them coloured energy to upgrade:
1st color determines weapon
- green: flamethrower
- red: laser
- blue: machine gun
- yellow: railgun
- cyan: bouncer
- magenta: rocket launcher

2nd color adds a bonus effect
- green: increases range
- red: increases damage
- blue: increases fire rate
- yellow: decreases energy cost
- cyan: freezes enemies
- magenta: burns enemies

Tips:
- Tap a tower to swap its two colours, long-press to eject one.
- Combine green/red/blue to get yellow/cyan/magenta, or find rare secondary colour crystals.
- You get 100% of your resources back when selling, and towers will eject their coloured energy, sell whenever you need to!
- Hold shift to keep building, or when building links, click and drag to build a line at max range
- Scroll to zoom in/out, Double tap and drag on mobile
- Click and drag from one link to another to force energy to take that route. Click and drag off to remove.
- Use 1-4 as hotkeys to build things, D to Sell.
- Use the button in the bottom left to speed up time.
